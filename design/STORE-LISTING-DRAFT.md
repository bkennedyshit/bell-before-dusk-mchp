## Short description

## Full description

A stolen seal has turned a guardian against the people it once protected.
As Yusuke, follow the thieves through a connected Japanese-inspired road adventure.
Explore Post Town, Cedar Hamlet and Willow Ferry, uncover the route of the stolen
cargo, and bring the ward seal home.

Switch between overhead exploration and side-scrolling platform trails. Climb
the bandit captain's tiered chamber, evade his marked attacks, and confront the
guardian at the Broken Ward. Optional favors earn alternate blades with different
reach, speed and power. Revisit discovered towns with unlocked town travel.

- Three towns, branching exploration roads, and five platform trails.
- Two boss encounters: the tiered captain arena and the overhead ward guardian.
- Three earned weapon styles; no paid combat advantages or consumable currency.
- Optional errands, saved progress and Normal/Assist combat settings.
- Touch joystick, tap movement, jump and slash controls.

Play the opening free. One purchase unlocks the remaining adventure; no subscription.
Google Play handles purchases and restoration. The game can run offline after its
assets are installed; purchase/restore checks require Google Play connectivity.
Progress is stored on your device, not in a game account or cloud-save service.

## Privacy disclosure draft — verify before publishing

The Android game stores gameplay progress and settings locally. It has no game
login, advertising SDK or enabled Vercel analytics on Android. Google Play Billing
processes the one-time unlock. The app queries purchase ownership and stores a
verified purchase record in app-private storage to support offline access. The game
does not receive payment-card details. Google processes purchase/account information
under its own policies. Local progress is not a transferable purchase entitlement.

Uninstalling or clearing app storage can remove local progress. Android system
backup behavior depends on device/account settings; do not promise zero data leaves
the device without auditing the selected backup configuration. Purchase restoration
does not restore gameplay progress. No current custom backend or telemetry endpoint
is part of the game. Re-audit if receipt verification or crash reporting is added.

The public policy must include the approved developer identity, support/privacy
contact and effective date. Those details are not invented here. Complete the Play
Data safety form against the final native dependencies and actual behavior, not this
draft alone.
