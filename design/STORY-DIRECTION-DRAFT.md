# Story direction — proposal, not an implemented rename

Archived proposal. The 0.3.0 authored narrative is now integrated; see
`STORY-FINAL.md`. The app title remains Bell Before Dusk.

Working title from the playtest: **Yusuke's Revenge**. The build remains named
Bell Before Dusk until the story/title is settled.

The most coherent expansion of what already exists: Yusuke is a courier whose
delivery seal is stolen during a coordinated robbery. The clapper thief is the
first lead, not the whole story. The gang uses stolen seals to close roads, seize
supplies and turn local guardians against their towns. Yusuke pursues the gang to
clear his name and reopen the route he was entrusted to protect.

Post Town supplies the introduction, original thief chase, inn and forge. Cedar
Hamlet reveals the stolen seal's purpose. Willow Ferry shows the consequences for
food and trade. Field side routes recover evidence, cargo and keys rather than
starting an unrelated resource-defense loop. The storehouse captain is the human
mid-boss; the Broken Ward guardian is the supernatural finale, freed after the
stolen seal is recovered.

This supports a town → field → town spine with optional platformer branches that
return to their field. New towns can extend the road graph later without replacing
the opening or removing prior levels. The current build has three towns, not a
completed fourth/coastal region. River March reuses the approved rice-road art
for now; distinct artwork and further towns are follow-up content work.

Not implemented by this document: a renamed app/store identity, rewritten full
dialogue, additional bosses/towns, premium chapter gates or billing.
