# Owner test — Android 0.3.1

Use the Android app, not the Vercel demo. Title must show Android 0.3.1.
Continue preserves your current journey. New Journey is the fresh-campaign option
and replaces that journey after its confirmation. Start on Normal in
JOURNAL → JOURNEY KIT → DIFFICULTY; Assist is available if you want it.

## Route to the ending

1. Post Town NORTH → Cedar Road northeast TRAILS → CEDAR CANOPY.
   Take the road token on the upper deck, then use the far EXIT on ground level.
2. Cedar Road NORTH → Cedar Hamlet. Talk to Aya. The upper-left forge sells the
   permanent blade upgrade; the inn heals. Return south to Post Town.
3. Post Town SOUTH → High Road EAST → Flooded Causeway. Cross and use EXIT.
   No mandatory marker or collectible exists on this crossing.
4. High Road SOUTH → Willow Ferry. Talk to Nao, heal at the inn, optionally buy
   the market's permanent health upgrade. Willow SOUTH → River March.
5. River March WEST → Harvest Levee. Get the upper-deck warehouse key; use EXIT.
6. Back through Willow, NORTH onto High Road, then WEST → Bandit Storehouse.
   Cross the approach, beat the human captain, then Cinder Maw in the tiered room.
   Use the far ground EXIT after both fights.
7. Return north through Post Town/Cedar Road to Cedar Hamlet. Talk to Aya;
   NORTH → Broken Ward. Defeat the guardian, read the ending and credits.

Optional original Forest Spirit Path is in Cedar Road's TRAILS menu. Shin's
tackle errand there unlocks the River Tanto. Takeshi's Causeway errand unlocks
the Warden Longblade. Accept first; hand in at Cedar Hamlet; equip in Journey Kit.
Closed eastern/coastal extensions are not required for the ending.

## Cinder Maw — learn the cues

- Arms up and floor glow: HOLD JUMP to land on a side ledge. Staying on ground
  takes damage. A jump with no ledge landing does not outlast the sustained fire.
- A horizontal row glows: get onto a DIFFERENT height. Jump up or hold the stick
  DOWN and tap JUMP to drop through a shelf. The marked row is the dangerous one.
- Landing ring: move away before the dive lands. It targets the marked surface,
  not your position after you have moved away.
- OPEN: approach, face the demon and SLASH. Its armor blocks damage outside
  recovery. All three attacks have warnings and a recovery window.

Captain victory is saved separately. If the demon kills you, you should restart
at the arena marker with full health and one mon lost, without repeating the
captain. Both victories must remain saved when you leave/relaunch the app.

## What to actively try

1. Tap versus hold JUMP. Climb low/middle/high ledges, drop through them, and
   walk off an edge. No invisible floor or unreachable required landing.
2. Move/stop on both sides of the demon. Front idle must change to the appropriate
   left/right attack image without white fragments, shrinking or floating feet.
3. Take a deliberate hit. HP must decrease; a successful dodge must avoid damage.
   Make a recovery-window hit and check its health bar actually decreases.
4. Die once. Check the arena retry, then win and use EXIT. The north ward gate
   must open, and going back into the completed storehouse must not force a repeat.
5. Pause mid-fight. HP/attacks must stop. Background/reopen the app and check that
   it stays paused until resumed. Relaunch after a clear to verify saved progress.
6. Play the remaining route without this sheet. Record any point where the guide,
   map, road label or gate sends you the wrong way.

For difficulty, report which attack hit you, whether the cue was clear, whether
you could reach safety in time, and whether the fight felt trivial, fair or
frustrating. We have functioning combat mechanics; final balance still requires
this player feedback. Note location, input, expected/actual behavior and whether
the issue repeats. Emulator results do not certify a physical phone.
